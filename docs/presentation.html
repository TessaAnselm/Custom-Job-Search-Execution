CareerPilot AI

Find relevant opportunities. Understand your fit. Prepare stronger applications—with the candidate still in control.

SayHi, I’m building CareerPilot AI, a job-search assistant designed to help people find relevant opportunities, match their experience to job requirements, and prepare tailored applications while keeping them in control.
01 · The problem

Less busywork.
More trust.

Job searching repeats the same time-consuming tasks for every opening.

SayWhy I built it: Job searching is repetitive and time-consuming. I wanted to automate the tedious parts without relying on AI to invent qualifications, change match scores, or submit applications without approval.

SayTechnology: CareerPilot builds on my existing Python and Flask application, using Playwright for job discovery, deterministic scoring for job matching, AWS Strands for agent orchestration, and vLLM serving Qwen3-4B on Akamai GPU infrastructure. Kubernetes supports deployment and access to the inference service, while Temporal remains optional for workflow management.

SayHow it works: Users upload a resume, verify their profile, and discover relevant jobs. CareerPilot explains matches, identifies skill gaps, and prepares tailored resumes and cover letters using verified information. Everything remains available for human review before applying.
04 · Self-hosted inference

Your app.
Akamai’s model.

The Flask app can run locally while inference requests reach Qwen3-4B on the workshop GPU.

SayWhy self-hosted inference matters: Instead of sending every AI request to an external provider, CareerPilot can use a model running on Akamai's GPU infrastructure, giving greater control over inference and the opportunity to measure performance and efficiency.
